# Briefs and handbacks are schemas enforced by the Works

A Wright knows only its brief and will confidently do the wrong thing if the brief is wrong, so the brief is a schema, not a convention. The Warden fills `dispatch_wright` with an objective, scope (in and out), constraints, inputs as pointers, a definition of done, a return shape and limits; the constraints field must be stated even when empty, and the Works rejects a brief missing any required field, adds the Guild's standards, and stores the rendered brief. A Wright returns a structured result (status done, blocked or failed; result pointer; evidence; out-of-remit observations; uncertainty), schema-enforced through the runner where supported, with large artifacts returned by pointer.

A Warden returns one inspected result to the Archwright: status done, partial or failed, result pointers, what it checked and rejected, the gate results the Works recorded, uncertainty, and flags (follow-up, decision-needed or risk, each with a one-line description and evidence pointer). Flags go to the Archwright only, which decides whether to open work for another Guild; Guilds never commission each other. A piece gets two rework rounds by default (configurable per Guild) before it returns as failed with its reason, last result and the Warden's diagnosis; a partial handback keeps the accepted pieces and parks the Job for the Archwright to decide.
