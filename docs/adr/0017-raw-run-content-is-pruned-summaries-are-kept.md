# Raw run content is pruned after 30 days; summaries are kept

The Works keeps two tiers. Jobs, Plans, tasks, briefs, handbacks, findings, asks and answers, gate results, permission posture, and usage and cost are kept indefinitely. Raw event content (messages, tool calls and outputs, raw vendor events), which may contain secrets and file contents, is pruned 30 days after its Job is accepted, discarded, failed or cancelled (configurable), leaving a marker so the dashboard says it was pruned. Pruning runs automatically at Works start and daily while it runs, never touches a Job with active runs, and compacts the database; `archwright prune --dry-run` shows what would go, the three most recent pre-migration database copies are kept and older ones deleted, and disk use (database and worktrees) is shown on the dashboard.

The developer can delete earlier: `archwright job purge <job>` removes a Job's raw content at once and keeps its summary, and `archwright job delete <job>` removes the Job entirely; both confirm first and are also dashboard commands executed by the Works. The factory does not redact secrets at ingestion in v1, since a best-effort filter would give false comfort and raw output is needed for debugging; protection is the 0700 directory, local-only storage and purge. Wright and Warden runs start ephemeral (Claude `--no-session-persistence`, Codex `--ephemeral`) so the Works holds the only copy, which suits resume being unused in v1; the Archwright is the developer's own interactive session and persists normally in the runner, which the factory does not manage, and `archwright doctor` reports a runner lacking an ephemeral option.
