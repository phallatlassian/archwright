# The Archwright starts Plan steps through `start_step`; approval only lifts the gate

Approving a Plan (ADR 0007) records the developer's go and tells the Archwright "work may be dispatched"; it starts nothing. A Plan step becomes work only when the Archwright calls `archwright tool start_step` with the Job and step id, which summons a fresh Warden for the Guild named in the step (ADR 0004). We chose an explicit Archwright call over the Works auto-starting unblocked steps on approval because ordering is a judgment (a Masonry step may need a linked writ, a step may need the output of an earlier one), and the Archwright is the one voice that holds the Plan (ADR 0001). The cost is that the next step waits until the Archwright reads its inbox after each handback: the Works nudges an idle Archwright session when it files the handback (ADR 0001), and a session that cannot be nudged waits in `read_inbox` with `wait_seconds` after `start_step` instead of ending its turn.

`start_step` refuses unless the Job is runnable (not paused, closed or parked), the Plan is cleared (`planCleared`), the step exists and is not running or done, and every step in its `blocked_by` has a handback. It renders a task brief from the step's title and `brief`, the Job's request, the Guild's charter and, once pieces were accepted, a pointer to them and the integration branch (`priorWork`; a step's Inputs are only labels), stores it, and starts a Warden run bound to the Job, the Guild and the step. The Warden then works only through the existing tools: `dispatch_wright`, `inspect_wright`, `accept_piece` and `handback`. The handback is filed in the Archwright's inbox, as dashboard command reports already are. Each step carries a status (pending, running, handed back, failed; ADR 0021 adds blocked and awaiting developer, and attempts) in the Works' store, so after a restart a running step's Warden is re-summoned from its task record and the Wright results recorded so far (ADR 0004). `start_jig` Plans use the same tool; a Jig Plan skips approval, not starting.

The Works carries the run through a `WardenRunner` interface with a fake for tests (ADR 0021 removes `ReviewRunner`, so every Warden run, review included, goes through it). Wardens and dispatched Wrights run on the real claude and codex adapters, on the runner their Guild is bound to and under the permission posture and never-list of ADR 0011 (`ARCHWRIGHT_WRIGHT_RUNNER=fake` selects the fakes for both). A task Warden works in a per-run directory the Works creates outside any Site (a review Warden uses the detached read-only worktree of ADR 0021), gets its Guild's skills like a Wright (ADR 0018), and its run is recorded with its timeline like any run.
