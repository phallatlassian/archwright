# Research covers codebase investigation through its Guild definition, not a new skill

Codebase investigation has no Pocock skill, and v1 writes none: the instruction lives in the Research Guild definition. `GUILD.md` carries the standard every finding meets, and two Wright brief templates carry the work: `wrights/docs.md` uses the upstream `research` skill unmodified for primary-source documents, and `wrights/code-trace.md` traces code paths and logs to establish a mechanism; the Warden skill chooses between them and splits the task.

The standard is: every claim carries evidence in a fixed form (code as `path:line` at a pinned commit SHA, docs as URL plus quote, runs as command plus output excerpt); each claim is labelled established, inferred or unknown; disagreement between sources is handed back as a disagreement, never silently resolved; recommendations sit in a separate labelled section and are never presented as findings; and what remains uncertain, and who or what could settle it, is stated. A code-trace Wright has its edit and write tools denied but may read, search and run non-mutating commands (git log or blame, grep, existing tests, a repro command) in its own throwaway detached worktree, within the developer's own runner rules, citing each run. Findings are stored in the Works with the Job, as a structured result plus the full cited Markdown, because a read-only Guild must not write into a Site; to keep one in a Site, the Archwright issues a separate Engineering task to commit it.
