# Every stage of a Job is a Plan step; review and the PR are steps, and there is no ReviewRunner

The Plan is the whole shape of a Job: any stage that does agent work is a step the Archwright starts with `start_step` (ADR 0020), so no stage hides outside the Plan, the caps or the dashboard. Review was the exception. It had its own start path (`request_review`), its own runner interface (`ReviewRunner`) and its own table, though a review differs from any other Warden run only in workspace kind, brief, tool role and result. We remove `ReviewRunner` and `request_review`; the `review` step of the `build` and `fix` Jigs is started like any other. Review's policy is kept and attaches to step start and completion, keyed by Guild access and result kind, not by the name `hallmark` (ADR 0009): a read-only detached worktree pinned to the reviewed SHA, a fresh Warden with no builder context, the verdict rules, stale detection and the PR go-ahead guard (ADR 0012).

A Guild manifest may declare a result kind. A Guild that declares `result: verdict` hands back through the one handback tool with the verdict shape (axes, findings, overall), and the rule that a handback needs at least one dispatched piece is relaxed for it (ADR 0006). The verdict rules stay Works code, applied when a verdict step completes. Handbacks and gate runs are keyed by step, not only by Job, so a review step can tell its blocker's handback from another Guild's. A step's blockers and checkpoints are enforced: `start_step` refuses until every blocker has a done handback whose gate run did not fail, and a checkpoint step waits for the developer.

A failed review does not rewrite the Plan. Rework is attempts of a fixed step: the Plan the developer approved stays as it is; a failed verdict re-opens the review's blocker as attempt 2 with the findings as constraints, then the review as attempt 2, and the Archwright still calls `start_step` for each. The Works limits attempts (once automatically, then the Job parks, as ADR 0012). We rejected new step instances (`build-2`, `review-2`) because they edit the Plan, and changing an ad hoc Plan resets its approval to pending, asking the developer for a go they already gave. Step statuses are pending, running, handed back, failed, and two more: blocked (a step's Warden waits on an open Ask) and awaiting developer (a checkpoint); the attempt number is a count, not a status.

Opening the PR is a step too. The `build` and `fix` Jigs end with an `open-pr` step blocked by `review`, started through `start_step`, and the PR go-ahead and the staleness check guard it. Warden runs, task and review alike, are shown in the queue and counted as active runs but sit outside the per-Site cap of 3, which stays about Wrights (ADR 0008). The cost: `start_step` carries more than ADR 0020 first described (step-keyed handbacks, attempts, workspace preparation, blocker and checkpoint rules), so it lands in two parts, steps other than review first. Evidence: `docs/research/review-as-a-plan-step.md`.

Amended by ADR 0029: a Step is carried out by the Job's standing Warden for that Guild, not a fresh Warden; Review's independence comes from sharing no context with the builder.
