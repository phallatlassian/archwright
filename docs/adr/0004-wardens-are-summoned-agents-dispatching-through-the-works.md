# Wardens are summoned per task and dispatch Wrights through the Works

A Warden is a fresh agent run summoned for each task the Archwright issues, not a standing process: two Jobs needing Masonry get two independent Wardens, and after a Works restart a Warden is re-summoned from its task record and the Wright results recorded so far. A Guild is a definition plus history in the Works. A Warden follows its Guild's skill (such as `implement-spec`, adapted) and does its own splitting, briefing and inspection, but dispatches every Wright through a Works tool (`dispatch_wright`, delivered as `archwright tool dispatch_wright`, ADR 0014) rather than native subagents, so ADR 0003's runner contract, event stream and per-Wright runner choice still hold, and the Works can inject the Guild's standards into every brief and cap parallelism centrally.

The Warden runs the deterministic gates itself, but the Works independently re-runs the Project's configured gates on the handback and records the result; the Archwright trusts the Works' record, not the Warden's claim. Wardens keep no hidden memory: anything worth keeping goes into the Project's steering files, and Guild standards live in the factory-owned Guild definition.
