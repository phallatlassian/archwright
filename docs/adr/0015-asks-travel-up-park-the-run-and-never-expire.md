# Asks travel up the layers, park the run, and never expire

When a Warden or Wright needs a human, it raises an ask through `archwright tool ask`; the Works records it and enforces the vertical path: a Wright's ask goes to its Warden, who answers from the brief, Guild or Site context if it can or escalates with its own ask to the Archwright, who puts it to the developer, and answers travel back the same way. Nothing waits in a live process: the run ends `blocked` with its questions (several at once, each with optional options and a recommended default), the Works parks the task, and after the answer starts a fresh run whose brief is the original brief plus the accumulated question-and-answer record, so asks survive restarts and day-long waits. Resuming the old session stays an optional runner capability, unused in v1.

Each ask is a record in the Works (id, Job, asker, questions, kind: decision, manual step or clarification, and whether it blocks). It appears in the Needs-you inbox, the Jobs board shows the Job as blocked on you, and the Archwright raises it at its next turn boundary; the developer answers in either place, the first answer wins and the other surface updates. Credentials and manual steps are described as a checklist the developer confirms, and secrets are never typed into an ask. Asks never expire and are never auto-answered, not even with the recommended default; other Jobs continue, and the developer can accept the default in one click, skip a question (the next brief says it is unanswered) or cancel the Job. Skills that only sharpen the developer's intent without reading a Site (`grilling`, `to-questionnaire`, checklists) run in the Archwright's own session; skills that need a Site (`grill-with-docs`, `domain-modeling`, `wayfinder`, `to-spec`, `to-tickets`, `triage`) run under a Wright with their asks relayed this way, which refines ADR 0005.
